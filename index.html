About

I'm an AI/ML Engineer at Cognizant, where I split my time between full-stack engineering on a production agentic RAG application and — earlier — building a multi-agent HR analytics platform from the ground up using LangGraph.

What pulls me in is understanding how these systems actually work underneath: retrieval, orchestration, access control, the failure modes of AI-generated code. I'd rather trace a bug back to its root cause than patch the symptom, and I use AI coding tools daily — but I review everything they produce line by line.

Outside work, I build personal projects around explainable, auditable AI: systems where the reasoning behind a decision is visible, not a black box.
